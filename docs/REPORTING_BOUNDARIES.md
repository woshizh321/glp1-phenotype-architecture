# Reporting boundaries

- No source or row-level data are redistributed.
- Participant identifiers and participant-level state vectors remain local.
- Taylor linearization, the official NHANES design variables, and beta/Korn–Graubard intervals are used.
- Structural N/A differs from not reported.
- Non-reportable numeric cells must remain blank and must not be reconstructed.
- Observable-signature equivalence is defined after NHANES projection and does not imply identical source trial eligibility criteria.
