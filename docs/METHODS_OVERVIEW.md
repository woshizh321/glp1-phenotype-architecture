# Methods overview

The workflow maps three quantitative clinical populations: diagnosed T2D for cardiovascular outcomes, BMI ≥25 kg/m² for overweight/obesity cardiovascular outcomes, and diagnosed T2D for kidney outcomes. The heart-failure layer is qualitative only.

Trial criteria are observed, proxy-observed, or unavailable. Branch and trial states collapse to Direct, Potential, Indeterminate, or Not aligned, with precedence Direct > Potential > Indeterminate > Not aligned. Analyses summarize trial-count depth, grouped depth, leave-one-trial-out breadth, cumulative accrual, observable signatures, and within-person cardiovascular–kidney representation.
